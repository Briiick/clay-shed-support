# Clay Shed support

Clay Shed is a studio notebook for potters. You can throw a pot by hand on a virtual wheel, design pots in a 3D studio, log every piece from planned to finished, and share them with other potters.

## Get help

[Open an issue](https://github.com/Briiick/clay-shed-support/issues/new) describing what happened, what you expected, and your iPhone and iOS version. Screenshots help. Please don't include passwords or API keys.

## Common questions

**Do I need an API key?**
Throw by hand, the Shelf, kiln presets and Community work without one. Only the AI design studio and photo labeling need one: they use your own Claude (Anthropic) or Gemini API key, which you add in Profile → Settings → AI keys. Claude keys come from the Claude Console. Concept photos need a Gemini key with billing turned on in Google AI Studio.

**Where is my key stored?**
Your key is encrypted in the app's database, tied to your account. The app only ever shows its last four characters. Remove it at any time in Settings → AI keys.

**How do I delete my account?**
Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your account, pots, photos, designs and saved keys are removed.

**Why can't other potters see my pot?**
Pots are private unless you mark them public on the pot's page.
