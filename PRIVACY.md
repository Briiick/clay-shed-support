# Clay Shed privacy policy

_Last updated: October 18, 2026_

Clay Shed is made by Alexander Bricken. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

## What Clay Shed stores
- **Account:** your email address, username and display name, and an optional avatar. Used to sign you in and show your profile.
- **Your pots and designs:** titles, notes, firing details, stages, photos you add, kiln presets, and Studio designs. Pots are private unless you make them public. Public pots, their photos and your profile can be seen by other Clay Shed users.
- **Follows, likes and saves:** so the community features work.
- **Your own AI keys, if you add them:** encrypted at rest, readable only by the app's server functions, and used only to make the requests you ask for.

## What Clay Shed does not do
- No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of your data for marketing.
- No analytics SDKs.

## Services the app uses
- **Supabase** hosts the database, file storage and sign-in.
- **Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Gemini)** receive a design prompt, a pot description or a photo, only when you use an AI feature and only with your own key. Their own privacy terms apply to that request.

## Deleting your data
Delete your account in Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your account, pots, photos, designs and saved keys. Leftover files are swept daily.
